HUMAN BOUNDARY

AI can research, cite, summarise and draft angles. It does not contact investors, send any email, state anything it cannot quote from a source, scrape LinkedIn, judge people or predict funding decisions.
Which angle to use, whether to take the meeting, and what to send afterwards, stays with Priya.
